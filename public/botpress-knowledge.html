US Pizza Ordering Knowledge Base

This document is the public information source for the US Pizza ordering website chatbot.

Ordering Flow

  1. Choose Delivery or Pickup.
  2. Choose an outlet. Customers can use their current location to sort outlets by estimated straight-line distance.
  3. Browse the menu, add items, and include special instructions or dietary restrictions for individual items.
  4. Review the cart and continue to payment.

Delivery requires the customer's name, unit/house/building, street address, postcode, city, and state. Pickup requires the customer's name.

Available outlets: US Pizza Klang; US Pizza Central i-City; US Pizza Bandar Baru Klang; US Pizza Shah Alam Seksyen 7; and US Pizza USJ 21.

Payment Methods

  • Cash on Delivery for delivery orders, or Cash on Pickup for pickup orders. Pay when the order is ready.
  • Online Transfer: complete the transfer through the customer's bank app.
  • DuitNow QR: scan the QR code with a banking app.
  • Touch 'n Go eWallet: pay with a Touch 'n Go eWallet.
  • GrabPay: pay with a GrabPay wallet.

Orders are estimated to be ready in 10 to 15 minutes after payment confirmation.

Food Information

US Pizza is halal certified. Pizzas use 100 percent premium cheese, original hand-tossed dough, fresh ingredients, US-formulated sauces, and an oil-free screen pizza process.

For allergens, dietary requirements, or preparation requests, customers should add a special instruction to the individual menu item before adding it to the cart. Special instructions are requests and should be confirmed with the selected outlet where necessary.
